GapMap, in one page

What happens between pressing Map the gaps and seeing the coloured rows — and where to look in the code.

Data flow

Follow one click

1
js/app.js → run() checks both boxes aren't empty, calls splitRequirements() and splitEvidence(), then waits for the model.
2
js/model.js → embed() turns every sentence into 384 numbers using the model files in /models and the WebAssembly runtime in /vendor. Nothing is downloaded from anywhere else.
3
js/engine.js → scoreMatches() compares each requirement with each CV line (dot product of normalised vectors + small keyword bonus), keeps the top 3, decides proven / partial / gap with statusFor(), and computes the fit.
4
js/app.js → render() and buildRow() draw the ring, rows and CV view. Clicking a row finds the CV line by data-line and highlights it.

Where to change things

Make GapMap stricter or more generous

Edit THRESHOLDS at the top of js/engine.js, then run npm test and map the sample again.

Teach it a new boilerplate section to ignore

Add words to SKIP_SECTION or BOILERPLATE in js/engine.js and add a test in tests/engine.test.mjs.

Change colours or fonts

All tokens are CSS variables at the top of css/style.css.

A practice to reuse

Before trusting an AI score, look at the raw numbers for a case you can judge yourself. Here that meant printing every requirement's best match for the sample and adjusting the rules (skip “About us”, rounded-% statuses) until the output matched human judgement — then locking it in with a test.
